# pakistan-auto-industry-analytics-bia
End-to-end big data analytics on 18 years of Pakistan auto industry data: PySpark ETL, star-schema data warehouse, Spark SQL analysis, KMeans clustering and sales forecasting.
Title: "Big data analytics, data warehousing and mining on 18 years of Pakistan auto industry data."
Dataset: PAMA monthly production and sales, July 2007 to May 2025.
Pipeline: load, clean, star-schema warehouse in Spark, Spark SQL analytics, KMeans, and a Random Forest forecast.
Key findings: two or three real results from your run, such as sales falling about 38% in FY2023 and total sales peaking in FY2018.
How to run: open in Colab, run all, upload the data file.
Limitations: a small dataset, a few unclassified models, and removed data errors.
Tools: Python, PySpark, Pandas, Matplotlib.
